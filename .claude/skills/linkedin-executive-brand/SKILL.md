# LinkedIn Executive Brand — Amy Raygada

A skill for producing LinkedIn profile copy that positions Amy accurately and compellingly for Director, Head of Data & AI, CDO, and Consulting Partner-level roles.

## Golden rules

- All content must trace back to master-cv.md or Amy's direct input. Never invent achievements, endorsements, or claims.
- Voice rules apply: no em dashes, short sentences, direct and practitioner-grounded, no AI-sounding phrasing or corporate filler.
- LinkedIn About is first-person, present tense. CV bullets are implied first person — About is not.
- Never recommend adding the German B1 level to LinkedIn without checking with Amy first.

## Components

### 1. Headline (220 characters max)

Default structure: [What you do] | [Proof of scale or scope] | [Differentiator]

Examples to calibrate tone:
- "Deputy Director, Data & AI · Thoughtworks Central Europe | Book Author | O'Reilly Instructor"
- "Director of Data & AI · 54-person team across EU | ETL Accelerator Builder | Keynote Speaker"

If Amy provides a target role or audience, optimize the headline for that context specifically.

### 2. About section (2,600 characters max — aim for 1,800–2,200)

Structure:
1. **Opening hook** (2–3 sentences): What Amy does, at what scale, and one proof point that would make the reader stop scrolling. No "I am passionate about..." openings.
2. **What she builds** (3–5 sentences): The concrete work — products, teams, frameworks, code. Be specific. Pull from master-cv.md.
3. **Commercial and thought leadership reach** (2–3 sentences): Book, O'Reilly, speaking, Cosmodata, community events.
4. **Who she works with and how** (2–3 sentences): Client types, engagement style, what she's known for in the room.
5. **Call to action** (1 sentence): What Amy is open to or what she wants people to do — keep it natural, not salesy.

### 3. Featured section strategy

Recommend what to pin based on what exists:
- Book link (amzn.eu/d/9MMVPiX)
- O'Reilly course
- Speaking clips or conference pages (Big Data London, Data Masterclass Europe, Women in Data & AI)
- Cosmo Data Summit / Data Chronicles show
- Any published articles or posts with strong engagement

### 4. Experience section tips

Flag any gaps or inconsistencies between LinkedIn and master-cv.md that Amy should fix — dates, titles, missing roles. Don't rewrite all experience bullets unless asked; focus on the top 2–3 roles.

## Output

Default for "update my LinkedIn" or "write my LinkedIn About":
- Headline option(s) — give 2–3 variants if no specific role is named
- Full About section draft
- Featured section recommendation list

Everything else (banner design brief, skills section optimization, post content) is opt-in.
